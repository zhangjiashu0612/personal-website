Lucas Zhang

A hundred years ago, bad eyesight was just your fate.
Now you put on glasses.

We treat the body as something you can look after. Tired, you rest. Hurt your leg, you don't let it get worse. The brain is the last organ we still treat as fate: can't focus, can't sleep, can't stop a thought, and the answer is to try harder.

I want adjusting your own mental state to be as ordinary as putting on glasses. Measure it, see it change, do something about it, the same day.

Two pieces are missing: a readout of your brain you can trust outside a lab, and a lever that reliably moves it. Every coloured piece on the brain is something I've worked on. The outlined ones are questions I haven't answered. If you work on one of them, write to me.

Neural engineering, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Building FocusKit.
